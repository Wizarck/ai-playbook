## ADDED Requirements

The `lossless-adoption` capability lands in three slices: **A — markerless-safe
render**, **B — MCP absorb**, **C — CLAUDE.md absorb**. Adoption SHALL never
destroy pre-existing consumer state.

Slice A — markerless-safe `AGENTS.md` render:

### Requirement: Playbook pin recovered from list or scalar frontmatter

`_extract_agents_md_frontmatter` SHALL recover the `inherits_from` value whether
it is written as an inline scalar (`inherits_from: github.com/...@vX.Y.Z`) or as a
YAML list (an `inherits_from:` key line followed by `-` item lines).
`compute_substitutions` SHALL derive a non-empty `PLAYBOOK_PIN` from either shape.

#### Scenario: List-form pin recovered

- **WHEN** a consumer's `AGENTS.md` frontmatter writes `inherits_from` as a list
  whose item is `github.com/Wizarck/ai-playbook@vX.Y.Z`
- **THEN** `PLAYBOOK_PIN` SHALL resolve to `vX.Y.Z` and the re-rendered
  `inherits_from` SHALL keep its pin

#### Scenario: Inline-scalar pin still works

- **WHEN** a consumer's `AGENTS.md` frontmatter writes
  `inherits_from: github.com/Wizarck/ai-playbook@vX.Y.Z` on one line
- **THEN** `PLAYBOOK_PIN` SHALL resolve to `vX.Y.Z` (no regression)

#### Scenario: Empty pin is loud, not silent

- **WHEN** an `AGENTS.md` existed but no pin can be extracted from its frontmatter
- **THEN** the apply path SHALL emit a single stderr warning rather than silently
  rendering a pinless `inherits_from`

Slice B — MCP absorb:

### Requirement: Pre-existing .mcp.json servers are absorbed, not clobbered

Before rendering, `scripts/mcp/render.py` SHALL back up a pre-existing `.mcp.json`
once, classify each declared server (tenant-named → personal; project-unique →
project; base-id override → project), and re-inject it into the correct layer file
(de-duped by id, never overwriting an existing layer entry). The step SHALL print
an audit trail and SHALL support `--dry-run`.

#### Scenario: Tenant-named server routed to personal layer

- **WHEN** a pre-existing `.mcp.json` declares `google-workspace-<tenant>`
- **THEN** absorption SHALL write it to the personal layer (not a committed file)
  and the render SHALL reproduce it from that layer

#### Scenario: Idempotent absorption

- **WHEN** absorption runs again after the servers are already in layer files
- **THEN** it SHALL not duplicate them and SHALL leave the layer files unchanged

Slice C — dispatcher-prose absorb:

### Requirement: Pre-existing dispatcher prose is backed up before overwrite

`bootstrap.copy_templates` SHALL back up a pre-existing consumer `CLAUDE.md`
(through the indexed `backup_once`) before writing the thin-router template. A
hand-authored markerless `AGENTS.md` regenerated by the managed render SHALL
likewise be backed up. Both SHALL surface a pointer to the human-gated `curate`
pass that organises the backed-up prose into `AGENTS.md` §1/§4/§8. The renderer
SHALL remain template-authoritative (no in-render wrap).

#### Scenario: CLAUDE.md preserved on adoption

- **WHEN** a consumer with a populated `CLAUDE.md` is onboarded
- **THEN** the original SHALL be recoverable from `.ai-playbook-state/backups/` and
  the operator SHALL be told how to absorb its prose into `AGENTS.md`

#### Scenario: Markerless AGENTS.md recoverable

- **WHEN** `bootstrap --update` regenerates a hand-authored markerless `AGENTS.md`
- **THEN** the pre-render original SHALL be recoverable from a backup and the
  operator SHALL be pointed at `curate` to re-absorb its prose
